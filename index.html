Business Problem

A major e-commerce platform operating across diverse Brazilian states faced visibility bottlenecks in monitoring regional fulfillment delays, customer attrition, and revenue concentrations. Leadership lacked an integrated analytics pipeline to evaluate customer lifetime value (LTV) and delivery logistics impacts on customer satisfaction.

Architecture & Technical Approach

  • Ingestion & Modeling: Loaded the 99K+ order Brazilian Olist dataset across 9 relational tables into a PostgreSQL database. Architected a star-schema data warehouse with a central fact_orders table linked to dimensions for customers, sellers, products, geolocation, and payments.
  • Python ETL: Formulated automated Python (Pandas) notebooks to validate data hygiene — handling null timestamps, deduplication, date parsing, and outlier reviews.
  • Advanced SQL Queries: Wrote modular SQL scripts leveraging Common Table Expressions (CTEs) and window functions (ROW_NUMBER(), DENSE_RANK(), LAG()) to calculate running revenue totals, repeat-purchase frequency, and average order value (AOV).
  • Power BI Dashboarding: Designed a 3-page executive report (Executive Summary, Customer Insights, and Fulfillment Operations) with dynamic DAX measures for Year-over-Year (YoY) revenue and retention tracking.

Key Insights & Business Recommendations

  • RFM Segmentation: Identified that the top 20% of customers ("Champions" & "Loyal") drove over 58% of gross revenue, establishing the priority cohort for VIP loyalty campaigns.
  • Delivery Delay Correlation: Uncovered that orders delivered past the estimated delivery date experienced a 1.8-point drop in review scores (from 4.2 to 2.4 stars). Provided recommendations to reroute logistics through localized regional distribution hubs.

Business Problem

Sales leadership relied on static, disparate weekly spreadsheets that required extensive manual compilation. The lag in reporting prevented agile forecasting, obscured product category margin disparities, and slowed executive regional reallocation.

Technical Solution

  • Automated Ingestion: Connected Power BI to SQL server tables and operational Excel logs via Power Query with pre-defined transformation rules. Reduced report generation time from 3 hours to 15 minutes.
  • DAX Formula Architecture: Authored custom time-intelligence DAX measures including TOTALYTD(), SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR(), dynamic profit margins, and trailing 3-month moving averages.
  • Interactive Visuals: Configured cross-filtering slices by fiscal quarter, geographic region, and product category alongside sales rep leaderboard benchmarks.

Business Impact

Delivered an intuitive executive cockpit that pinpointed the top revenue-generating territory ($6.2M in North America) and exposed lagging profitability in select accessory lines, enabling leadership to protect a 40% target gross profit margin.

Business Problem

High corporate turnover rates were generating unsustainable recruitment costs and institutional memory loss. Human Resources required deep visibility into retention patterns across departments, salary bands, and career tenure brackets.

Technical Implementation

  • Data Consolidation: Merged employee master records, performance appraisals, and exit interviews from SQL databases and HR spreadsheets.
  • Risk Modeling: Constructed a retention risk matrix plotting employee performance rating against tenure and compensation bands ($40k–$60k seeing 16% peak attrition).
  • Demographic Profiling: Built visual breakdowns for department attrition (Sales leading at 14.2%, followed by Engineering at 12.1%), gender distribution, and age groupings.

Strategic Outcomes

Pinpointed that 64% of resignations occurred within the first 24 months of tenure. Armed with this insight, HR leadership instituted a 90-day structured onboarding check-in program and salary adjustments for the highest flight-risk brackets, reducing early turnover.
